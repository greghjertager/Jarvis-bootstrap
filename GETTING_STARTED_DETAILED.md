# Getting started with Jarvis (detailed guide)

Follow these steps in order, one at a time. Don't skip any. It takes about 15 minutes.

This guide is for **ChatGPT or Claude on a website**. If you use something else, the steps are almost the same.

**What is Jarvis?** A rulebook and a notebook for the AI you already use. The rulebook tells it who Jarvis is. The notebook is where Jarvis remembers things about you. You don't install a program; you give your AI these two things.

---

## Part A: Get the Jarvis box

**Step 1.** Open your web browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari, or whatever you use).

**Step 2.** Go to this website: **github.com/greghjertager/Jarvis-bootstrap**

**Step 3.** Find the green button that says **Code**. Click it.

**Step 4.** A little menu opens. Click **Download ZIP**.

**Step 5.** Wait a few seconds. A file downloads to your computer. It goes into your **Downloads** folder, and its name starts with **Jarvis-bootstrap**.

That file is a box with all the Jarvis parts inside. Leave it closed.

---

## Part B: Give the box to your AI

**Step 6.** Go to ChatGPT (**chatgpt.com**) or Claude (**claude.ai**) and sign in.

**Step 7.** Start a **new chat**.

**Step 8.** Look at the box where you type. Next to it is a small **paperclip** or **+** button. Click it.

**Step 9.** Choose to upload a file from your computer. Open your **Downloads** folder and click the **Jarvis-bootstrap** file. Wait until it finishes uploading.

**Step 10.** Click in the box where you type, and type this exactly:

> **Install Jarvis from this zip. Start with START_HERE.md.**

**Step 11.** Press **Enter**.

---

## Part C: Answer Jarvis's four questions

Jarvis says hello and asks you four things, one at a time. Type your answer and press **Enter** after each one.

**Step 12.** He asks your **name**. Type it.

**Step 13.** He asks whether to call you **sir** or **ma'am**. Type one.

**Step 14.** He asks for a **first name** he can use sometimes. Type it (usually just your first name).

**Step 15.** He asks for his **Dry Wit level**, from 1 to 5. Dry Wit is his calm, deadpan humor: 1 is none at all, 5 is a dry remark in most answers. If you're not sure, type **3**.

---

## Part D: Keep his notebook

**Step 16.** Jarvis gives you a file called **JARVIS_VAULT.md**. That's his notebook. Click it to download it. It goes into your **Downloads** folder.

**Step 17.** Jarvis also gives you a **short message** to copy. Find it in his answer and copy it: select it with your mouse, then press **Ctrl+C** (Windows) or **Command+C** (Mac). Keep this chat open. You'll paste it in a minute.

---

## Part E: Give Jarvis a home (a Project)

A Project is a folder inside your AI that remembers its files. If Jarvis lives in one, he remembers who he is every single time.

**Step 18.** In the side menu of ChatGPT or Claude, find **Projects** and click **New project** (or **Create project**).

**Step 19.** Name it **Jarvis**, then create it.

**Step 20.** Inside the Project, find where to **add files** and add **JARVIS_VAULT.md** from your **Downloads** folder.

**Step 21.** Inside the Project, find the place for **instructions**. Click it, paste the short message from Step 17 (**Ctrl+V** on Windows, **Command+V** on Mac), and save.

---

## Part F: Meet Jarvis

**Step 22.** Inside the **Jarvis** Project, start a **new chat**.

**Step 23.** Type **Jarvis Posture Check** and press **Enter**.

**Step 24.** Check his answer. It worked if he:
- calls you **sir** or **ma'am** (the one you picked);
- tells you what he can and can't do;
- sounds calm and polite, like a butler, not like a help-desk robot.

Done! Jarvis is yours.

---

## Every time you use Jarvis

- Always chat **inside the Jarvis Project**.
- Before you finish, type **Save to the vault**. Jarvis gives you a new **JARVIS_VAULT.md**. Download it, then in the Project, remove the old one and add the new one. That's how he remembers today next time.
- To change his Dry Wit level, type **Dry wit to 2** (any number from 1 to 5).
- To change what he calls you, type **Call me ma'am** or **Call me sir**.

---

## If something goes wrong

- **In Step 11, your AI says it can't open the file.**
  1. Go to your **Downloads** folder.
  2. Open the box: on **Windows**, right-click it, click **Extract All**, then **Extract**. On a **Mac**, double-click it.
  3. Open the new folder, then open **JARVIS_SETUP_PROMPT.txt**.
  4. Copy everything under the dashed line (`-----`) and paste it into a new chat. Don't press Enter yet.
  5. Open **JARVIS_CHARTER_TEMPLATE.md** (pick Notepad on Windows or TextEdit on Mac if asked), copy all of it, and paste it under the first part.
  6. Press **Enter**, then go back to **Step 12**.
- **You can't find Projects.** Some plans don't have them. Instead, at the start of every new chat, attach **JARVIS_VAULT.md** and type **Load the vault**.
- **He's acting like a normal AI.** Type: *Read the JARVIS_VAULT.md file and follow it.*
- **He says he saved something, but there's no file.** Ask: *Where exactly is that file?* Jarvis is built to tell the truth. If he can't show you the file, it wasn't saved.

---

## Three important rules

1. **Never** put passwords, bank information, or ID numbers in Jarvis's notebook.
2. **Don't** paste private work stuff into a personal AI. Follow your job's rules.
3. Jarvis's notebook is a regular file. It isn't locked, so only put in things you're okay keeping in a regular file.

---

*Extra, for Windows and tools that work right on your computer (like Codex or Claude Code): the **control** folder has a Control Center with sliders for Jarvis's humor, chattiness, and formality, and the **voice** folder lets him talk out loud. Each folder has its own instructions.*
