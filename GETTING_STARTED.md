# How to get Jarvis

**Super simple. Just do each step, in order.**

You need two things: a computer, and an account with an AI you like to use (ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot, Gemini, or another).

---

### Get Jarvis

**1.** Click this link: **[github.com/greghjertager/Jarvis-bootstrap](https://github.com/greghjertager/Jarvis-bootstrap)**

> You'll see a page full of file names. Ignore them.

**2.** Click the green button that says **Code**.

**3.** Click **Download ZIP**.

> A file downloads. That file is Jarvis.

---

### Give Jarvis to your AI

**4.** Go to your AI's website and log in. For example:

- ChatGPT: **[chatgpt.com](https://chatgpt.com)**
- Claude: **[claude.ai](https://claude.ai)**
- Copilot: **[copilot.microsoft.com](https://copilot.microsoft.com)**
- Gemini: **[gemini.google.com](https://gemini.google.com)**

**5.** Click the **+** (or the paperclip) next to the box where you type.

**6.** Click **Upload from computer** (or **Add photos and files**).

**7.** Click **Downloads**. Click the file that starts with **Jarvis-bootstrap**. Click **Open**.

**8.** Click the box where you type. Type this:

> **Install Jarvis from this zip. Start with START_HERE.md.**

**9.** Press **Enter**.

---

### Answer four questions

**10.** Jarvis asks your **name**. Type it. Press **Enter**.

**11.** He asks: **sir or ma'am?** Type one. Press **Enter**.

**12.** He asks for a **first name** to call you sometimes. Type it. Press **Enter**.

**13.** He asks for his **Dry Wit level**, from 1 to 5. Type **3**. Press **Enter**.

> Dry Wit is his calm, deadpan humor. 1 is none at all. 5 is a dry remark in most answers.

---

### Save Jarvis

**14.** Jarvis gives you a file called **JARVIS_VAULT.md**. Click it to download it.

> This file is Jarvis's memory. Don't delete it.

**15.** Jarvis gives you a short message in a box. Click the **copy** button on that box.

**16.** On the left side of your AI's screen, click **Projects**, then **New project**.

**17.** Name it **Jarvis**. Click **Create**.

**18.** Click **Add files**. Click **Downloads**. Click **JARVIS_VAULT.md**. Click **Open**.

**19.** Click **Instructions**. Press **Ctrl+V** (on a Mac: **Command+V**). Click **Save**.

---

### Say hi

**20.** Inside the **Jarvis** project, start a **new chat**.

**21.** Type **Jarvis Posture Check**. Press **Enter**.

**22.** If he calls you **sir** or **ma'am**, you did it. Jarvis is yours.

---

### Every time after that

- Open the **Jarvis** project and chat there.
- When you're done, type **Save to the vault**. Download the new **JARVIS_VAULT.md** he gives you, and put it in the project instead of the old one.

---

### Stuck?

- **No Projects button?** Every time you start a new chat, attach **JARVIS_VAULT.md** and type **Load the vault**.
- **It won't take the file in step 7?** Use the **[detailed guide](GETTING_STARTED_DETAILED.md)**.
- **Something else?** Ask whoever sent you this link.
