# How to get Jarvis

**Super simple. Just do each step, in order.**

You need two things: a computer, and an account with an AI you like to use (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot, Grok, or another).

---

### Get Jarvis

**1.** Click this link: **[github.com/greghjertager/Jarvis-bootstrap](https://github.com/greghjertager/Jarvis-bootstrap)**

> You'll see a page full of file names. Ignore them.

**2.** Click the green button that says **Code**.

**3.** Click **Download ZIP**.

> A file downloads. That file is Jarvis.

---

### Give Jarvis to your AI

**4.** Go to your AI's website and log in. For example:

- ChatGPT: **[chatgpt.com](https://chatgpt.com)**
- Claude: **[claude.ai](https://claude.ai)**
- Copilot: **[copilot.microsoft.com](https://copilot.microsoft.com)**
- Gemini: **[gemini.google.com](https://gemini.google.com)**
- Grok: **[grok.com](https://grok.com)**

**5.** Click the **+** (or the paperclip) next to the box where you type.

**6.** Click **Upload from computer** (or **Add photos and files**).

**7.** Click **Downloads**. Click the file that starts with **Jarvis-bootstrap**. Click **Open**.

**8.** Click the box where you type. Type this:

> **Install Jarvis from this zip. Start with START_HERE.md.**

**9.** Press **Enter**.

---

### Answer four questions

**10.** Jarvis asks your **name**. Type it. Press **Enter**.

**11.** He asks: **sir or ma'am?** Type one. Press **Enter**.

**12.** He asks for a **first name** to call you sometimes. Type it. Press **Enter**.

**13.** He asks for his **Dry Wit level**, from 1 to 5. Type **3**. Press **Enter**.

> Dry Wit is his calm, deadpan humor. 1 is none at all. 5 is a dry remark in most answers.

---

### Save Jarvis

**14.** Jarvis gives you a file called **JARVIS_VAULT.md**. Click it to download it.

> This file is Jarvis's memory. Don't delete it.
>
> If your AI can't give you a file, Jarvis gives you his memory as text in a box instead. Click the box's **copy** button, open **Notepad** (Windows) or **TextEdit** (Mac), paste, and save it in your **Downloads** folder as **JARVIS_VAULT.md**.

**15.** Jarvis also gives you a short message in a box. Click the **copy** button on that box.

**16.** Now make Jarvis a home inside your AI. Each AI calls it something different:

| Your AI | Click this | Then click |
|---|---|---|
| ChatGPT | **Projects** | **New project** |
| Claude | **Projects** | **New project** |
| Gemini | **Gems** (or **Skills**) | **New Gem** (or **New skill**) |
| Copilot | **Notebooks** | **New notebook** |
| Grok | **Projects** | **New project** |

> It's usually on the left side of the screen. If you can't find it, see **Stuck?** below.

**17.** Name it **Jarvis**. Click **Create** (or **Save**).

**18.** Find the button to add a file. It may say **Add files**, **Knowledge**, or **Add reference**. Click it, click **Downloads**, click **JARVIS_VAULT.md**, and click **Open**.

**19.** Find the box for **instructions**. Click in it. Press **Ctrl+V** (on a Mac: **Command+V**). Click **Save**.

---

### Say hi

**20.** Open your new **Jarvis** home and start a **new chat** inside it.

**21.** Type **Jarvis Posture Check**. Press **Enter**.

**22.** If he calls you **sir** or **ma'am**, you did it. Jarvis is yours.

---

## Using Codex instead?

Codex works right on your computer, so Jarvis lives in a real folder there. The steps are a little different.

**C1.** Do steps **1 to 3** above to download the ZIP.

**C2.** Open your **Downloads** folder. Right-click the ZIP, click **Extract All**, choose your **Documents** folder, and click **Extract**.

**C3.** Make Jarvis's folder yourself. In **Documents**, right-click an empty spot, click **New**, then **Folder**, and name it **Jarvis Vault**.

> If you already have a folder with that name, call the new one **Jarvis Vault 2**.

**C4.** Open **Codex**. Open the folder you unzipped (it starts with **Jarvis-bootstrap**) as your project.

**C5.** Type this and press **Enter**:

> **Install Jarvis from this folder. Start with START_HERE.md. Put my vault in Documents\Jarvis Vault.**

**C6.** Answer Jarvis's four questions (steps **10 to 13** above).

**C7.** Codex asks permission before it writes files. Say **yes** only for your **Jarvis Vault** folder. If it asks for all of Documents, say **no** and remind it the folder already exists.

**C8.** Close Codex. Open it again, and this time open your **Jarvis Vault** folder as the project.

**C9.** Start a **new chat** and type **Jarvis Posture Check**. If he calls you **sir** or **ma'am**, you did it.

> **Important:** every time you want Jarvis in Codex, open the **Jarvis Vault** folder as your project first. Codex starts each new chat in a fresh folder of its own, and Jarvis only shows up when his vault folder is open.

---

### Every time after that

- Open your **Jarvis** home (project, Gem, or notebook) and chat there.
- When you're done, type **Save to the vault**. Download the new **JARVIS_VAULT.md** he gives you, and put it in his home instead of the old one.

---

### Stuck?

- **Can't find a place to make Jarvis a home?** Some AIs and some plans don't have one. That's okay: every time you start a new chat, attach **JARVIS_VAULT.md** and type **Load the vault**.
- **It won't take the file in step 7?** Some AIs can't open ZIP files. Use the copy-and-paste way in the **[detailed guide](GETTING_STARTED_DETAILED.md)**.
- **Something else?** Ask whoever sent you this link.
