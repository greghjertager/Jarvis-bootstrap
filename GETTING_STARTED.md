# How to get Jarvis

**Super simple. Just do each step, in order.**

You need two things: a computer, and an account with an AI you like to use.

> Buttons sometimes look a little different from what's written here, because these apps change. If yours doesn't match exactly, look for the closest one.

---

## Part 1: Get Jarvis (everyone does this first)

**1.** Click this link: **[github.com/greghjertager/Jarvis-bootstrap](https://github.com/greghjertager/Jarvis-bootstrap)**

> You'll see a page full of file names. Ignore them.

**2.** Click the green button that says **Code**.

**3.** Click **Download ZIP**.

> A file downloads into your **Downloads** folder. Its name starts with **Jarvis-bootstrap**. That file is Jarvis.

---

## Part 2: Pick your AI and follow its steps

- **[ChatGPT](#chatgpt)**
- **[Claude](#claude)**
- **[Gemini](#gemini)**
- **[Copilot](#copilot)**
- **[Grok](#grok)**
- **[Codex](#codex)** (works right on your computer)

Using something else? Try the ChatGPT steps. They're close for most AIs.

### ChatGPT

**1.** Go to **[chatgpt.com](https://chatgpt.com)** and log in.

**2.** Click the **+** next to the box where you type, then **Add photos and files**.

**3.** Click **Downloads**. Click the **Jarvis-bootstrap** file. Click **Open**.

**4.** Type this, then press **Enter**:

> **Install Jarvis from this zip. Start with START_HERE.md.**

> If ChatGPT says it can't open the ZIP, see **[If your AI won't take the ZIP](#if-your-ai-wont-take-the-zip)**.

**5.** Jarvis asks you four questions. Answer each one, then press **Enter**:
- your **name**;
- **sir** or **ma'am**;
- a **first name** to call you sometimes;
- your **Dry Wit level** from 1 to 5 (calm, deadpan humor: 1 is none, 5 is a dry remark in most answers). Not sure? Type **3**.

**6.** Jarvis gives you a file called **JARVIS_VAULT.md**. Click it to download it. This is his memory. Keep it.

**7.** Jarvis gives you a short message in a box. Click the **copy** button on that box.

**8.** In the left sidebar, click **Projects**, then **New project**. Name it **Jarvis**. Click **Create project**.

**9.** Inside the project, click **Add files**. Click **Downloads**, then **JARVIS_VAULT.md**, then **Open**.

**10.** Inside the project, click **Instructions**. Press **Ctrl+V** (on a Mac: **Command+V**). Click **Save**.

**11.** Start a **new chat** inside the **Jarvis** project. Type **Jarvis Posture Check** and press **Enter**.

**12.** If he calls you **sir** or **ma'am**, you did it.

**Every time after that:** open the **Jarvis** project and chat there.

### Copilot

Copilot can't open ZIP files, so you'll give it two files instead.

**1.** Open your **Downloads** folder. Right-click the **Jarvis-bootstrap** ZIP and click **Extract All**, then **Extract**.

> On a Mac, just double-click the ZIP. A new folder appears with the Jarvis files inside.

**2.** Go to **[copilot.microsoft.com](https://copilot.microsoft.com)** and log in.

**3.** Click the **+** (or the paperclip) next to the box where you type, then **Upload**.

**4.** Open the folder you just unzipped. Hold down **Ctrl** (on a Mac: **Command**) and click both **JARVIS_SETUP_PROMPT.txt** and **JARVIS_CHARTER_TEMPLATE.md**. Click **Open**.

**5.** Type this, then press **Enter**:

> **Install Jarvis. Read JARVIS_SETUP_PROMPT.txt and follow it, using JARVIS_CHARTER_TEMPLATE.md.**

**6.** Jarvis asks you four questions. Answer each one, then press **Enter**:
- your **name**;
- **sir** or **ma'am**;
- a **first name** to call you sometimes;
- your **Dry Wit level** from 1 to 5. Not sure? Type **3**.

**7.** Jarvis gives you his memory, called **JARVIS_VAULT.md**. If it's a file, click it to download it. If it's text, see **[If your AI gives you text instead of a file](#if-your-ai-gives-you-text-instead-of-a-file)**.

**8.** Jarvis gives you a short message in a box. Copy it: select it with your mouse and press **Ctrl+C** (on a Mac: **Command+C**).

**9.** In the left menu, click **Notebooks**, then **New notebook**. Name it **Jarvis**.

> Don't see **Notebooks**? Some Copilot accounts don't have them. Skip to **[No place to make Jarvis a home?](#no-place-to-make-jarvis-a-home)**

**10.** In the notebook, click **Add reference** (or **Upload**), and pick **JARVIS_VAULT.md** from **Downloads**.

**11.** In the notebook, find **Custom instructions**. Click in the box, press **Ctrl+V** (on a Mac: **Command+V**), and save.

**12.** Start a chat in the **Jarvis** notebook. Type **Jarvis Posture Check** and press **Enter**.

**13.** If he calls you **sir** or **ma'am**, you did it.

**Every time after that:** open the **Jarvis** notebook and chat there.

### Codex

Codex works right on your computer, so Jarvis lives in a real folder there.

**1.** Open your **Downloads** folder. Right-click the **Jarvis-bootstrap** ZIP, click **Extract All**, choose your **Documents** folder, and click **Extract**.

**2.** Make Jarvis's folder yourself. In **Documents**, right-click an empty spot, click **New**, then **Folder**, and name it **Jarvis Vault**.

> If you already have a folder with that name, call the new one **Jarvis Vault 2**.

**3.** Open **Codex**. Open the folder you unzipped (it starts with **Jarvis-bootstrap**) as your project.

**4.** Type this, then press **Enter**:

> **Install Jarvis from this folder. Start with START_HERE.md. Put my vault in Documents\Jarvis Vault.**

**5.** Jarvis asks you four questions. Answer each one, then press **Enter**:
- your **name**;
- **sir** or **ma'am**;
- a **first name** to call you sometimes;
- your **Dry Wit level** from 1 to 5. Not sure? Type **3**.

**6.** Codex asks permission before it writes files. Say **yes** only for your **Jarvis Vault** folder. If it asks for all of Documents, say **no** and remind it that the folder already exists.

**7.** Close Codex. Open it again, and this time open your **Jarvis Vault** folder as the project.

**8.** Start a **new chat**. Type **Jarvis Posture Check** and press **Enter**.

**9.** If he calls you **sir** or **ma'am**, you did it.

**Every time after that:** open your **Jarvis Vault** folder as the project first. Codex starts each new chat in a fresh folder of its own, and Jarvis only shows up when his vault folder is open.

## Help

### If your AI won't take the ZIP

**1.** Open your **Downloads** folder. Right-click the **Jarvis-bootstrap** ZIP and click **Extract All**, then **Extract**. (On a Mac, double-click it.)

**2.** In your AI, click the **+** or paperclip to upload files. Open the new folder, hold down **Ctrl** (on a Mac: **Command**), and click both **JARVIS_SETUP_PROMPT.txt** and **JARVIS_CHARTER_TEMPLATE.md**. Click **Open**.

**3.** Type this, then press **Enter**:

> **Install Jarvis. Read JARVIS_SETUP_PROMPT.txt and follow it, using JARVIS_CHARTER_TEMPLATE.md.**

**4.** Go back to your AI's steps above, at the four questions.

### If your AI gives you text instead of a file

**1.** Click the **copy** button on the box with Jarvis's memory (or select it all and press **Ctrl+C**, on a Mac **Command+C**).

**2.** Open **Notepad** (Windows) or **TextEdit** (Mac).

**3.** Press **Ctrl+V** (on a Mac: **Command+V**) to paste.

**4.** Click **File**, then **Save As**. Choose your **Downloads** folder, name it **JARVIS_VAULT.md**, and click **Save**.

> On a Mac, in TextEdit, click **Format**, then **Make Plain Text** before saving.

### No place to make Jarvis a home?

Some AIs and some plans don't have Projects, Gems, or Notebooks. That's okay. Every time you start a new chat, attach **JARVIS_VAULT.md** and type **Load the vault**.

### Saving what Jarvis learns

When you're done for the day, type **Save to the vault**. Jarvis gives you a new **JARVIS_VAULT.md**. Download it, then put it in his home (project, Gem, or notebook) instead of the old one. (In Codex, he saves it himself.)

### Something else?

- **He's acting like a normal AI.** Type: *Read the JARVIS_VAULT.md file and follow it.*
- **He says he saved something, but you can't find it.** Ask: *Where exactly is that file?* Jarvis is built to tell the truth. If he can't show you, it wasn't saved.
- **Still stuck?** Use the **[detailed guide](GETTING_STARTED_DETAILED.md)**, or ask whoever sent you this link.

## Three important rules

1. **Never** put passwords, bank info, or ID numbers in Jarvis's memory.
2. **Don't** paste private work stuff into a personal AI. Follow your job's rules.
3. Jarvis's memory file is **not locked** (not encrypted), so only put things in it that you're okay keeping in a regular file.
